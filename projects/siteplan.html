ZER KEYZ Academy — Site Plan

Site Name

ZER KEYZ Academy — Chosen to represent structured, accessible piano and keyboard instruction focused on technique, chords, rhythm, and expressive performance.

Site Purpose

Provide beginners and intermediate players with clear learning tracks, filterable lessons with progress tracking, and an interactive practice planner and enrollment form.

Target Audience

Aspiring pianists and keyboardists—from absolute beginners learning hand posture and scales to developing players practicing chord voicings, rhythm, and keyboard sound layering.

Color Schema

  • Primary & Surface Ink (#14171f): Header, footer, and primary headings/text for high contrast.
  • Background & Cards (#f8f7f4 / #ffffff): Clean light background and elevated cards for readability.
  • Primary Accent (#1d4ed8) & Warm Accent (#b45309): Primary buttons, active navigation state, and metadata highlights.

Typography

  • Headings: Outfit (Google Fonts) for bold section and page titles.
  • Body & UI: Plus Jakarta Sans (Google Fonts) for clear body copy, labels, and lesson descriptions.

Wireframe & Page Structure

Pages: index.html (Home), lessons.html (Filterable Lessons & Completion Tracker), enroll.html (Enrollment & Practice Planner Form), and references.html (Citations).

Mobile Layout (< 768px): Single-column stacked cards with a collapsible hamburger navigation menu.

Desktop Layout (≥ 768px): Horizontal navigation bar, two-column hero section, and multi-column lesson and form layouts.
